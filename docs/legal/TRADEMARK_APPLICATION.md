## 4. Mark Description and Distinctive Elements

**Mark Analysis:**
- "QR" refers to Quick Response codes, a widely recognized technology term
- "callbox" suggests a communication system for requesting assistance
- Combined as "QRcallbox" creates a distinctive mark for QR-based assistance systems
- The mark is suggestive rather than merely descriptive, as it requires imagination to understand the connection between QR codes and customer assistance

**Distinctiveness Argument:**
The mark QRcallbox is inherently distinctive as it creates a unique association between QR code technology and customer assistance systems that goes beyond mere description of the services.
