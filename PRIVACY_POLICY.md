# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 2025  
**Version:** 2.1.0

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how QRcallbox ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our service. By using QRcallbox, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: First name, last name, email address, phone number, store number, job title
- **Authentication Data**: Password (encrypted), email verification status
- **Profile Changes**: Requested changes to account information pending approval

### Usage Data
- **QR Code Interactions**: Store locations, area descriptions, timestamps of assistance requests
- **Response Tracking**: Associate response times, responder names, message content
- **Analytics Data**: Usage patterns, feature interactions, performance metrics
- **Session Information**: Login times, device information, browser type

### Technical Data
- **IP Addresses**: Collected for security, fraud prevention, and terms acceptance tracking
- **Device Information**: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
- **Cookies**: Session cookies for authentication and user preferences
- **Server Logs**: Access logs, error logs, performance monitoring data

### Integration Data
- **GroupMe Integration**: Group IDs, message content, user IDs, webhook data
- **Third-party Services**: Data from integrated platforms as configured by administrators

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Purposes
- **Service Operation**: Enable QR code generation, customer assistance notifications, and response tracking
- **User Management**: Account creation, authentication, approval workflows
- **Analytics**: Generate insights on response times, popular areas, and system performance
- **Communication**: Send assistance notifications, system updates, and support responses

### Business Operations
- **Legal Compliance**: Maintain records for terms acceptance, audit trails
- **Security**: Fraud prevention, abuse detection, system security monitoring
- **Improvement**: Enhance features, optimize performance, develop new capabilities
- **Support**: Provide customer service, troubleshoot issues, respond to inquiries

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Internal Sharing
- **Administrators**: Approved administrators can access user management and system analytics
- **Associates**: Response data is shared to facilitate assistance and track performance
- **Support Team**: Access to necessary data for troubleshooting and assistance

### Third-Party Services
- **Firebase/Google Cloud**: Hosting, database, authentication, and analytics services
- **GroupMe API**: Message delivery and webhook processing (when enabled)
- **Analytics Providers**: Anonymized usage data for service improvement

### Legal Disclosure
We may disclose your information when required by:
- Legal process (subpoenas, court orders)
- Law enforcement investigations
- Protection of rights, property, or safety
- Business transfers (mergers, acquisitions)

## Data Security

### Protection Measures
- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit and at rest using industry standards
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication requirements
- **Regular Audits**: Security assessments and vulnerability scanning
- **Secure Infrastructure**: Firebase security features and Google Cloud Platform protections

### Your Responsibilities
- Maintain secure passwords and account credentials
- Report suspected security breaches immediately
- Keep contact information current for security notifications
- Use official QRcallbox domains and applications only

## Data Retention

### Retention Periods
- **Account Data**: Retained while account is active, plus 7 years after deletion
- **Usage Logs**: 3 years for analytics, 1 year for detailed logs
- **Response Data**: 5 years for performance analytics and legal compliance
- **Support Tickets**: 3 years after resolution
- **Terms Acceptance**: Permanent retention for legal compliance

### Data Deletion
- Account deletion removes personal identifiers but preserves anonymized analytics
- Users can request specific data deletion subject to legal retention requirements
- Automatic purging of expired data according to retention schedules

## Your Privacy Rights

### Access and Control
- **View**: Access your personal information through account settings
- **Correct**: Update account information (subject to administrator approval)
- **Export**: Request a copy of your personal data in portable format
- **Delete**: Request account deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)

### Preferences
- **Communications**: Opt out of non-essential notifications
- **Analytics**: Request exclusion from certain analytics (where technically feasible)
- **Integrations**: Control third-party service connections

### Limitations
Some data cannot be deleted due to:
- Legal compliance requirements
- Fraud prevention needs
- System integrity requirements
- Ongoing business relationships

## Cookies and Tracking

### Cookie Types
- **Essential**: Required for login, security, and basic functionality
- **Functional**: User preferences, language settings, theme choices
- **Analytics**: Usage patterns, performance monitoring (anonymized)
- **Security**: Fraud detection, abuse prevention, session protection

### Your Choices
- Browser settings can control cookie acceptance
- Essential cookies cannot be disabled without losing functionality
- Third-party cookie controls through browser or platform settings

## Children's Privacy
