Frequently Asked Questions

Account & Access

How do I create an account?

New accounts require administrator approval. Sign up through the registration page, and an admin will activate your account within 24-48 hours. You'll receive an email notification when approved.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. The link expires after 1 hour.

Why is my account pending approval?

All new accounts require administrator verification to ensure security. This process typically takes 24-48 hours. Contact support if it's been longer.

Notifications

I'm not receiving notifications. What should I check?

Verify these settings:

  • Your shift status is set to "On Shift"
  • Notifications are enabled in your account settings
  • Browser/device notifications are allowed
  • Your FCM token is active (visible in Settings > Debug Info)
  • For Android: Check battery optimization settings aren't blocking the app

Can I customize which notifications I receive?

Yes! Go to Settings to configure notification preferences, including GroupMe integration, Do Not Disturb settings, and specific notification types.

What is the "Respect Do Not Disturb" setting?

When enabled, the app won't send notifications during your device's Do Not Disturb hours. Emergency notifications may still come through based on administrator settings.

QR Codes & Scanning

How do I generate a QR code for my store?

Administrators can generate QR codes through the Admin Panel > QR Code Management. Specify the area description and download the printable QR code.

A QR code was scanned but I didn't get notified. Why?

Common reasons:

  • You're not marked as "On Shift"
  • Your store number doesn't match the scanned code's location
  • Another associate already responded
  • Notifications are disabled in your settings
  • Network connectivity issues

Can customers see who responded to their request?

No. Customer-facing pages only show that assistance is on the way. Employee names are visible only to internal users.

GroupMe Integration

How do I connect my GroupMe account?

Navigate to Admin Panel > GroupMe Integration, click "Connect GroupMe," authorize the app, and create a bot for your store's group.

My GroupMe bot isn't sending messages. What's wrong?

Check these:

  • Bot is still in the GroupMe group (not removed)
  • OAuth token hasn't expired (reconnect if needed)
  • Bot is associated with the correct store number
  • GroupMe API service is operational

Analytics & Reporting

Where can I see my response time statistics?

Access the Insights tab to view your average response time, total assists, and leaderboard rankings for your store.

Can I export analytics data?

Administrators have access to data export features through the Admin Panel > Analytics section. Data can be exported as CSV for further analysis.

Common Issues & Solutions

Login Problems

Issue: "Invalid credentials" error

Solution:

  • Verify email and password are correct (passwords are case-sensitive)
  • Clear browser cache and cookies
  • Try password reset if you're unsure of your password
  • Ensure your account has been approved by an administrator

Issue: "Account pending approval" message

Solution:

  • Wait for administrator approval (typically 24-48 hours)
  • Contact your store manager or administrator
  • Email support if it's been longer than 48 hours

Notification Issues

Issue: Delayed or missing notifications

Solution:

  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify you're marked as "On Shift" in Settings
  • Ensure notification permissions are granted in browser/device settings
  • For Android: Disable battery optimization for QRCallBox app
  • For iOS: Check notification settings in iOS Settings > QRCallBox
  • Try logging out and back in to refresh your FCM token

Issue: Receiving notifications for wrong store

Solution:

  • Verify your store number in Settings > Profile
  • Request profile change if incorrect (requires admin approval)
  • Contact administrator to correct your store assignment

Mobile App Issues

Issue: Android app not installing or updating

Solution:

  • Enable "Install from Unknown Sources" in Android settings
  • Download the latest APK from: qrwebaccdb.web.app/app/
  • Ensure you have sufficient storage space
  • Uninstall old version before installing new version if auto-update fails

Issue: iOS TestFlight build not available

Solution:

  • Install the free TestFlight app from the App Store
  • Open TestFlight and sign in with your Apple ID (same one you use for App Store)
  • Check your email for the beta invitation from QRCallBox
  • Tap the invitation link or enter the invite code in TestFlight
  • If no invitation received after 48 hours, contact support at sinaptick@gmail.com

Remember: TestFlight uses your existing Apple ID - no separate account needed!

Performance Issues

Issue: App running slowly or freezing

Solution:

  • Clear browser cache (Web: Settings > Clear browsing data)
  • Close unnecessary tabs/apps
  • Restart the app or refresh the page
  • Update to the latest version
  • Check system requirements are met

Escalation Procedure

When to Escalate

  • No response within stated response time for your priority level
  • Issue marked as resolved but problem persists
  • Security-related concerns requiring immediate attention
  • Critical business impact affecting multiple stores/users

How to Escalate

  1. Reply to your original support ticket with "ESCALATION REQUESTED"
  2. Email sinaptick@gmail.com with "URGENT ESCALATION" in subject line
  3. Include your original ticket number and reason for escalation
  4. Provide updated information about business impact

Emergency Support

For critical system outages or security incidents outside business hours, email sinaptick@gmail.com with "EMERGENCY" in the subject line. Include your contact phone number for immediate callback.
