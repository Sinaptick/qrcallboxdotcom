Account Deletion via Email

Step 1: Submit Deletion Request

Send an email to our support team with the following information:

To: sinaptick@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - QRcallbox

Include in email:

  • Your full name
  • Email address associated with your account
  • Store number (if applicable)
  • Reason for deletion (optional but helpful)
  • Confirmation statement: "I understand this action is permanent"

Email template example:

Subject: Account Deletion Request - QRcallbox

Hello,

I would like to delete my QRcallbox account.

Full Name: [Your Name]
Email: [your.email@example.com]
Store Number: [Your Store #]
Reason: [Optional]

I understand this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Thank you,
[Your Name]

Step 2: Identity Verification

For security purposes, we will verify your identity before processing the deletion:

  • Confirmation email sent to your registered email address
  • You must respond from the same email within 48 hours
  • Additional verification may be required for admin accounts

Step 3: Processing & Confirmation

Once verified, we will:

  • Process your deletion request within 5 business days
  • Send final confirmation email when deletion is complete
  • Your account will be immediately inaccessible
  • Backend data purge occurs within 30 days

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your QRcallbox account, the following information will be permanently removed:

Personal Information

  • Account profile (name, email, phone number, job title)
  • Login credentials and authentication data
  • Store assignments and access permissions
  • User preferences and settings
  • FCM tokens and device registrations

Activity Data

  • Response history and assistance records
  • Personal analytics and performance metrics
  • Support tickets submitted by you
  • Work schedule configurations

Integration Data

  • GroupMe bot connections and OAuth tokens
  • Workvivo integration settings
  • Third-party service authorizations

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I delete my account instantly?

Yes! Log in to your account and visit this page again to delete your account immediately without waiting for email verification.

Will deleting my account affect other users at my store?

No. Deleting your account only removes your personal data. Other users will continue to have full access to the system. If you're the only admin, notify your store manager to assign a new admin before deletion.

Can I recover my account after deletion?

No. Account deletion is permanent. You would need to create a new account, which requires administrator approval and starts fresh with no previous data.

How long does the deletion process take?

If you're logged in, deletion is immediate. Via email: account deactivation occurs within 5 business days, with complete data purge within 30 days.

What if I'm having issues and considering deletion?

Please contact support first at sinaptick@gmail.com. Many issues can be resolved without deleting your account. We're here to help!
